# Code of conduct

Baton adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project
space, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, and own your mistakes.
- No harassment, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, trolling, or publishing
  others' private information.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private reporting:
[github.com/kanumuri9593/Baton/security/advisories/new](https://github.com/kanumuri9593/Baton/security/advisories/new),
with "Code of conduct" in the title. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer will review
each report and may take any action the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe, from a
warning to a ban from the project.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is at
[contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
